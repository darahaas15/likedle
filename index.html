Likedle

Guess songs from your own Liked Songs

Likedle is a Songless-style game: hear a tiny snippet, guess the track, unlock more seconds with every miss. It plays only songs you have liked on Spotify.

One-time setup: create a (free) Spotify developer app so Likedle can read your library. Takes about two minutes.

  1. 1
    Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. 2
    Click Create app. Name and description can be anything (e.g. "Likedle").
